Six ways to tell it

The gospel doesn’t change, but the path through it does. These are the six frameworks people actually use — what each one says, in what order, and which verse carries each step.

    The shared spine

    Line the six frameworks up and the same handful of verses keeps reappearing. That overlap is the actual argument — the frameworks differ in shape, not in content. Romans 3:23 appears in all six; Romans 5:8 and Romans 6:23 in five.

    Responding: the sinner’s prayer

    Most of these frameworks end by inviting a response, and the response is often put into words as a prayer. GotQuestions is blunt that the prayer itself is not the mechanism: “Saying a sinner’s prayer will not accomplish anything on its own.” It’s an expression of faith already placed in Christ, not a formula that produces it.

    “God, I know that I am a sinner. I know that I deserve the consequences of my sin. However, I am trusting in Jesus Christ as my Savior. I believe that His death and resurrection provided for my forgiveness. I trust in Jesus and Jesus alone as my personal Lord and Savior. Thank you Lord, for saving me and forgiving me! Amen!”

    Sample wording · GotQuestions.org

    Before you use one

    • A framework is scaffolding, not a script. Every one of these was built to help you remember the order of the argument under pressure — not to be recited at someone.
    • Start where the person actually is. The Bridge and the Four Laws open with God’s love; the Romans Road and 3 Circles open with the problem. Someone who doesn’t think they have a problem needs the first kind of opening.
    • Two of these are drawings. The Bridge and 3 Circles are meant to be sketched on a napkin while you talk. That’s the point — the other person watches it take shape, and can draw it back for someone else.
    • The Wordless Book is for children. Five colors, no text. It’s the one framework here that works without literacy.
    • Leave room for the conversation. Every step above is a place to stop and ask what they think, not a checkpoint to clear.
